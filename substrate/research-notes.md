# Substrate Research Notes

This page records research questions that grow from the Substrate concept inside
CGF.

The goal is to keep CGF self-contained. Research notes should introduce ideas
as part of this project from their first readable version.

## Initial Questions

- What is the smallest useful definition of a graph-shaped computational
  subject?
- How should each of the 9 core primitives be declared clearly?
- What makes a subject complete enough to leave Substrate and move toward
  Architecture or Applications?
- What validation discipline should every Substrate example carry?
