# Research

Research preserves theory, studies, references, and claim boundaries behind
CGF.

Research can be deeper and messier than public entry docs. Public docs should
link into research only after the simple explanation is clear.

This folder is self-contained. It develops CGF concepts as ideas that grow from
this project.

Start with [../FOUNDATION_TRAVERSAL.md](../FOUNDATION_TRAVERSAL.md) when you
need to choose a CGF mix. Start with [research-program.md](research-program.md),
then use [coverage-map.md](coverage-map.md) to see where each research area
belongs.

Current research maps:

- [coverage-map.md](coverage-map.md): coverage map for CGF research areas by
  layer, maturity, and next destination.
- [connection-map.md](connection-map.md): explicit Substrate, Research, and
  Labs links for current cards, studies, evals, and claim boundaries.
- [geometric-multimodal-framework.md](geometric-multimodal-framework.md):
  geometric structure, symmetry, topology, dynamics, and multimodal
  representation.
- [higher-dimensional-graphs.md](higher-dimensional-graphs.md):
  topological domains, higher-dimensional cells, boundary maps, and
  Hodge-style structure.
- [substrate-research-map.md](substrate-research-map.md): map of the Substrate
  research threads.
- [derivative-mechanics-study.md](derivative-mechanics-study.md): deeper
  research direction for deriving equations, operators, residuals, and solver
  choices from complete Substrate subjects.
- [extension-studies/README.md](extension-studies/README.md): deeper research
  pages for optional Substrate extension components.
- [application-class-studies.md](application-class-studies.md): pressure tests
  for field-theoretic, operator-learning, physical-computation, and other
  subject families. Basis-mode now has a dedicated extension study linked from
  the extension studies index.
- [references.md](references.md): research status types, public reference queue, and
  internal research status rules.
